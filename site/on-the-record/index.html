The listing rule

Published here before anyone is listed, the same way the scorecard's criteria were published before anything was graded:

  • A person is listed if and only if a public, linkable commitment in their own words exists — their statement, their website, their recorded remarks. Every entry links to its source.
  • Entries appear in chronological order of commitment and show name, chamber or candidacy, party, district, commitment date, and the source link. Nothing else.
  • An entry is removed if its commitment is publicly retracted.
  • There is no other column. No "opposes," no "undecided," no "declined to respond," ever. This page records commitments; it does not grade people. Grading is for ballot questions.
