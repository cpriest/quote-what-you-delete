What the amendment does
Minn. Const. art. XI, §8 struck = deleted · underlined = added
The principal of the permanent school fund shall be perpetual and inviolate forever.
…to secure the maximum return consistent with the maintenance of the perpetuity of the fund.
…all losses not offset by gains shall be repaid to the fund from the interest and dividends earned thereafter.
…and managed as a perpetual financial resource…
Reasonable and necessary administrative costs associated with managing the fund or school trust lands may be paid from the fund in the manner prescribed by law.
The distribution policy shall be prescribed by law…

"Perpetual" survives as a goal, not a rule. The struck text ordered losses repaid. The new text sets a management aim and leaves the rest to statute.

The payout rate isn't in the amendment. The new 4.5% distribution sits in ordinary statute. A future legislature can change it by simple majority.
